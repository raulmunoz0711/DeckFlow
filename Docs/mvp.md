# 1. Minimum viable outcome; the smallest thing that works end to end and your client would use
A card scanner that identifies the card, and is able to use physcial errors on the card in order to give the card a grade [Grading of a card refers to giving the card a rating.]

# 2. At least one non-goal; something you are deliberately not building
We are not trying to replace the act of sending in cards to get graded, we are just giving unofficial grades using systems that are comparable to these grading companies in order to  determine whether it would be worth while for the client to send in a card.

# 3. An assumption that would kill the project, and how would you test
That our model will not be able to recreate and remain constistent with the grading of trusted companies such as PSA or TAG.

That our model will be able to view the card, and determine phsysical errors through basic cameras (phone, latop, etc.)
